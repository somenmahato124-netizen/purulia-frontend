Badhna Parab

🐄 Bandhna (Badna) Parab – Festival of Cattle & Gratitude

Bandhna Parab (lokally Badna Parab) holo Purulia o Paschimanchal er ekta prachin folk utsob, jekhane goru, chagol aar palito pashu der pujo kora hoy. Gramanchaler lokera ei din pashu der sajay, snan koray aar notun rope, phool & alpona diye bandhon kore. Ei parab krishi jiboner sathe manusher gobhir jogajog-er protik. 🌾🐂

Best Time: Kali Pujor Por (October – November) 🍂

Best Moment: Morning Rituals & Village Activities 🌅

Famous For: Cattle Worship • Rural Culture • Folk Rituals

Why Bandhna (Badna) Parab is Famous?

  • ✔️ Unique Festival Dedicated to Cattle 🐄
  • ✔️ Strong Connection with Agriculture 🌾
  • ✔️ Traditional Village Rituals & Beliefs 📿
  • ✔️ Colorful Decoration of Animals 🎨
  • ✔️ Deep Respect for Nature & Livelihood ❤️

Before You Experience Bandhna Parab

Bandhna Parab mainly rural area te palon kora hoy. Ei din goru der kono kaj kora hoy na — shudhu pujo, khabar aar bhalo basha. Gram er prottek goru sajano thake aar puro village e ekta anondo o shanti-r poribesh thake. ✨

📍 Location: Rural Areas of Purulia, West Bengal

🎉 Festival Time: October – November

⚠️ Note: Animal care & local customs respect korun 🙏
