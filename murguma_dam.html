Murguma Dam

About Murguma Dam

Murguma Dam is located at the foothills of Ayodhya Hills, Purulia. Surrounded by forests, hills and tribal villages, it is one of the most peaceful and scenic tourist destinations in Purulia.

The dam is built on the Saharajhore River and considered a promising tourist spot for camping, photography, nature lovers and weekend trips.

🌟 Why Murguma Dam is Famous?

Murguma Dam is famous for its stunning natural beauty, peaceful environment and the scenic backdrop of the Ayodhya Hills. The combination of water, mountains, forests and tribal culture makes it a unique destination in Purulia.

  • ✔️ Ayodhya Hills & Forest Valley View
  • ✔️ Perfect Spot for Photography & Pre-wedding Shoots
  • ✔️ Camping, Trekking & Nature Trails Available
  • ✔️ Quiet and Peaceful – Ideal for Weekend Getaways
  • ✔️ Beautiful Sunrise & Sunset View Points
  • ✔️ Lake Boating Experience
  • ✔️ Tribal Village Culture & Local Handicrafts
  • ✔️ Bird Watching & Wildlife Spotting Opportunities

🧭 How To Reach

Nearest Railway Station: Jhalda (16 km)

Nearest Town: Begunkodor (6 km)

Bus/Car Route: Purulia → Begunkodor → Murguma
