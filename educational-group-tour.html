Educational Group Tour

Purulia, West Bengal

Tour Overview

Our Educational Group Tour in Purulia is specially designed for schools, colleges, universities, and educational institutions. The tour focuses on nature study, geography, tribal culture, historical places, and outdoor learning experiences in a safe and well-managed environment.

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Local Study Tour

Arrival at Purulia / Barabhum Railway Station → Hotel / hostel check-in → Visit Ajodhya Hills → Nature study session → Evening cultural interaction → Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 2: Waterfalls & Culture

Breakfast → Visit Bamni / Turga / Usri Waterfall → Tribal village interaction → Lunch → Return journey → Drop at station.
