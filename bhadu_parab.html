Bhadu Parab

🌸 Bhadu Parab – Festival of Love & Folk Songs

Bhadu Parab holo Purulia, Bankura o Paschimanchal er ekta sundor folk utsob, jekhane gramanchaler meye ra Bhadu Devi’r gaan geye pujo kore. Ei parab mainly monsoon & autumn er somoy palon kora hoy aar meyeder folk gaan, rangin saj-sajja aar utsobmukhor poribesh ei parab ke alada charm dey. 🎶🌼

Best Time: Bhadro Mash (August – September) 🌧️🍂

Best Moment: Evening Folk Songs & Village Gatherings 🌆

Famous For: Bhadu Songs • Folk Rituals • Village Culture

Why Bhadu Parab is Famous?

  • ✔️ Traditional Folk Festival of Purulia 🌸
  • ✔️ Melodious Bhadu Folk Songs 🎶
  • ✔️ Women-centric Cultural Celebration 👩‍👧‍👧
  • ✔️ Beautiful Rural Decorations & Rituals 🎨
  • ✔️ Deep Emotional Connection with Village Life ❤️

Before You Experience Bhadu Parab

Bhadu Parab mainly gramanchal-e palon kora hoy, especially meyeder madhyame. Bhadu gaan gaoa, local rituals aar sheshe simple immersion ei utsob-er main attraction. Ei parab Purulia’r folk music & emotion-er shundor porichoy. ✨

📍 Location: Rural Areas of Purulia, West Bengal

🎉 Festival Time: Bhadro Mash (Aug–Sep)

⚠️ Note: Local traditions & emotions respect korun 🙏
