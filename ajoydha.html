Ajoydha Pahar

Ajoydhya Pahar - The Crown of Purulia

Ajoydhya Hills is one of the most beautiful attractions in Purulia district, known for its scenic forests, rocky cliffs, tribal villages, amazing waterfalls and clear lakes. This is a perfect destination for nature lovers, adventure seekers and photographers. The sunrise & sunset view from the hilltop is just magical!

Best Time to Visit: October to February ❄️

Activities: Trekking, Camping, Photography, Boating, Rock Climbing 🔥
