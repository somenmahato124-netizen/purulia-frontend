Living Tribal Heritage & Folk Traditions

Famous Festivals of Purulia

Immerse yourself in the rhythmic beats of Dhamsa-Madol, vibrant Chhau masks, Jhumur folk songs, and age-old harvest celebrations of Manbhum.

April – June (Chaitra)

Chhau Dance Festival

World-famous UNESCO-recognized semi-classical martial folk dance depicting mythological stories with elaborate handcrafted Charida masks and energetic acrobatics.

Read Story Charida & Baghmundi
Dec – Jan (Makar Sankranti)

Tusu Parab

A joyous winter harvest festival celebrated throughout the month of Poush, featuring soulful Tusu folk songs, colorful Chowdol processions, and river immersion on Makar Sankranti.

Read Story Harvest Festival
Aug – Sept (Bhadra)

Bhadu Parab

A cherished monsoon festival dedicated to Princess Bhadravati of Kashipur, celebrated with melodious Bhadu folk songs, traditional dances, and floral offerings.

Read Story Kashipur Heritage
Sept (Bhadra Ekadashi)

Karam Parab

A sacred nature-worship and harvest festival dedicated to Karam Devta, symbolizing youth, fertility, and prosperity, accompanied by night-long Jhumur and Karam dances around the sacred Karam branch.

Read Story Nature Worship
Oct – Nov (Kartik Amavasya)

Badna Parab

A multi-day agrarian and cattle-worship festival celebrated by Santhal, Kudmi, and Kurmi communities during Kali Puja, featuring colorful Sohrai wall paintings, Ahira songs, and Goru Khunta.

Read Story Sohrai & Cattle Festival
