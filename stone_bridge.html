Stone Bridge

🌉 Stone Bridge – Hidden Heritage of Purulia

Stone Bridge holo Purulia’r ekta old-style stone construction bridge, jekhane history, nature ar simple rural surrounding mile ekta unique scenic view create kore. Ei bridge ta monsoon time e aro beshi sundor lage, jokhon niche nodi ba jheel e jol bhore thake. Heritage lover, photographer & traveler der jonno eta ekta must-visit spot. 🏞️

Best Time: Monsoon & Winter (July – February) 🌧️❄️

Best Moment: Morning Light & Evening Golden Hour 🌅

Famous For: Stone Architecture • Nature View • Photography

Why Stone Bridge is Famous?

  • ✔️ Old Stone Architecture & Heritage Feel 🪨
  • ✔️ River / Water Flow View Below 🌊
  • ✔️ Perfect for Photography & Reels 📸
  • ✔️ Calm & Less Crowded Spot 🍃
  • ✔️ Surrounded by Green Nature

Before You Visit

Stone Bridge e jawar rasta generally bike & car friendly, kintu monsoon time e slippery hote pare. Bridge er pashe bose nature enjoy kora, nodi’r sound shona ar photography korar jonno eta ekta perfect shanto jayga. ✨

🚘 Distance: Near Purulia Rural Area

📍 Location: Stone Bridge Area, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Monsoon e sabdhane chalaben ⚠️
