Rich Culture of Purulia

Purulia is considered the cultural heart of West Bengal, known for its ancient heritage, tribal traditions, folk art, music, festivals, and nature-based lifestyle. Here, culture is not just celebrated—it is lived every day through dance, craft, spiritual beliefs, and community values.

Key Cultural Highlights

  • Purulia Chhau Dance: A UNESCO-recognized martial-art based performance with masks & mythological storytelling.
  • Chau Mask Art – Charida: Handcrafted masks used in Chhau performances; a globally known art tradition.
  • Jhumur Folk Music: Traditional melodies expressing love, life, hope & the bond with nature.
  • Tribal Heritage: Santhal, Munda, Ho & Kurmi culture rooted in nature, community & spirituality.
  • Handicrafts & Rural Art: Bamboo, terracotta, clay, wooden craft & tribal jewelry shaping identity.

Festivals that Define Purulia

• Tusu Parab

A riverside winter folk festival of music, hope & prayer.

• Bhadu Festival

Women-led celebration of storytelling, tradition & cultural values.

• Bandna Parab

Honoring cattle, nature & rural livelihood through ritual art.

• Karam Parab

A tribal nature-worship festival representing unity & respect for earth.

Cultural Identity

The culture of Purulia is powerful because it blends spirituality, tradition, art, and nature. It stands as one of the richest cultural destinations of India.
