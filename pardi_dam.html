Pardi Dam - Calm Nature of Ajodhya Hills

Pardi Dam holo Ajodhya Pahar er ekta shanto o green landscape-er water spot, jekhane pahar, jheel aar hawa mile ekta peaceful environment toiri hoy. Sunrise & sunset e ei jayga aro jhorjhore hoye uthhe. Family outing, photography, reel & relaxing time er jonno perfect. 💧🌄

Best Time: Winter & Monsoon (August – February) 🌿

Best Moment: Sunrise & Sunset View 🌅

Famous For: Calm Water + Green Valley + Less Crowd 🍃

Why Pardi Dam is Famous?

  • ✔️ Water + Hills + Green Valley View
  • ✔️ Peaceful & Less Crowded Spot
  • ✔️ Photography & Reel Friendly 📸
  • ✔️ Walking & Nature Time 🌿
  • ✔️ Best for Calm Sitting & Relaxation

Before You Visit

Pardi Dam e jawar raasta smooth & bike/car friendly. Jheel er pashe local sitting spot ache jekhane bose hawa khawa jaye. Monsoon e water level beshi & view onek sundor hoy. ✨

🚘 Distance: Ajodhya Hills Region

📍 Location: Pardi Village Side, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Evening e besh calm spot — group hole valo 🌙
