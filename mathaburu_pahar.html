Mathaburu Pahar - Cloud Touching Hills

Mathaburu Pahar Ajodhya Hills er ekta silent o scenic hilltop point jekhane cloud layer, forest valley aar hawa mile ek onyo rokom shanti pawa jay. Trekking, hiking & photography lovers der jonno eita swargor moto ek spot. 🏔️🌿

Best Time: Winter & Cloudy Season (September – February) ☁️

Best Moment: Early Morning View + Cloud Touching Peaks 🌄

Famous For: Trekking Trail, Hilltop View, Peaceful Nature 🍃

Why Mathaburu Pahar is Special?

  • ✔️ Hilltop Trekking Route & Viewpoint
  • ✔️ Cloud Touching Peaks & Forest View ☁️🌲
  • ✔️ Drone Shot, Reel & Travel Photography Friendly 📸
  • ✔️ Natural Silence & Calm Environment 🕊️
  • ✔️ Perfect For Nature Lovers & Peace Seekers

Before You Visit

Mathaburu point e jawar raasta trekking type — comfortable shoe pora valo. Jodi early morning jaw, cloud er niche hill dekha jay, ja onek rare beauty. Choto group hole aro safe & enjoyable. 🌫️✨

🚶‍♂️ Difficulty: Easy to Moderate Trekking

📍 Location: Ajodhya Hills, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Night e ekdom silent area — group e jawar suggestion 🌙
