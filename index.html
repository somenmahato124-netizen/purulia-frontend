About The Land

Nature, Culture & Heritage

Located in the westernmost part of West Bengal on the Chota Nagpur Plateau, Purulia is known for its rolling hills, tranquil dams, ancient heritage, and vibrant tribal traditions.

From red Palash blossoms in spring to energetic Chhau performances under open skies, Purulia offers an unforgettable experience for every traveller.

Travel Information

Essential tips for planning your Purulia trip

🚆

How to Reach

Direct trains from Howrah/Kolkata to Purulia Jn, Barabhum, or Adra (approx. 5–6 hrs).

🏨

Where to Stay

Eco-resorts, hilltop cottages & homestays available in Ayodhya Hills, Baranti & Murguma.

🍛

Local Food

Taste authentic Bengali & tribal dishes, Desi chicken curry, Pitha, and Posto delicacies.

📅

Best Time to Visit

October to March for pleasant weather & blooming red Palash flowers; Monsoon for waterfalls.
