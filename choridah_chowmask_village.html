Chowmask Village

🌾 Choridah Chowmask Village – Cultural Heart of Ajodhya Hills

Choridah Chowmask Village holo Ajodhya Pahar er ekta peaceful tribal village, jekhane green valley, lok sanskriti, madol & traditional life mile ekta asli Purulia vibe toiri hoy. Ekhane nature + culture duitar experience ek sathe paoa jay, specially festival time e atmosphere onek sundor. 🍃🪘

Best Time: Winter & Festival Season (Tusu, Chhau) ✨

Best Moment: Evening Cultural Vibe & Village Walk 🌄

Famous For: Tribal Culture + Nature Village + Traditional Lifestyle

Why Choridah is Famous?

  • ✔️ Tribal Culture & Local Tradition 🪘
  • ✔️ Chhau & Folk Dance Vibe During Festivals 🎭
  • ✔️ Mountain + Village Nature Experience 🌿
  • ✔️ Photography & Reel Friendly Background 📸
  • ✔️ Calm Area with Less Crowd & Peace 🕊️

Before You Visit

Choridah road easy & travel-friendly. Village er moddhei traditional culture feel kora jay, local der sathe kotha hole experience aro bhalo lage. Photography & walking er jonno perfect peaceful jagah. ✨

🚘 Distance: Ajodhya Hills Region (Near Roadside Villages)

📍 Location: Choridah Chowmask Village, Ajodhya Pahar, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Local culture ke respect kore photography korun 🙏
