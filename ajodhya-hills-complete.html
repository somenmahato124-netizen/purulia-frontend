Ajodhya Hills Complete Circuit Tour

Ajodhya Hills Region, Purulia

Tour Overview

Explore the untouched natural beauty of Ajodhya Hills with this complete circuit tour. Perfect for nature lovers, families, and adventure seekers, this trip covers scenic hilltops, roaring waterfalls, tranquil dams, dense Sal forests, tribal villages, and breathtaking sunrise & sunset viewpoints.

Tour Highlights

⛰️ Ajodhya Hills View Points
🌄 Mayur Pahar
🗿 Mathaburu Pahar
🦅 Pakhi Pahar Rock Art
💦 Turga Dam & Waterfall
🌊 Bamni Waterfall
🌲 Upper & Lower Ajodhya Forest
🌅 Sunrise & Sunset Points

Detailed Itinerary

Day 01

Arrival & Ajodhya Hills

Pick up from Purulia / Barabhum Railway Station → Drive to Ajodhya Hills → Visit Upper & Lower Forest and scenic View Points → Sunset Point → Hotel check-in, dinner & overnight stay.

Day 02

Complete Circuit Sightseeing

Breakfast → Mayur Pahar → Mathaburu Pahar → Turga Dam → Turga Waterfall → Bamni Falls → Pakhi Pahar → Evening leisure & local cultural walk → Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 03

Sunrise & Departure

Early morning sunrise view → Breakfast → Local sightseeing & Charida Mask Village visit → Drop at Purulia / Barabhum Railway Station.
