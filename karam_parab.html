Karam Parab

🌿 Karam Parab – Festival of Nature, Faith & Prosperity

Karam Parab holo Purulia o Chotanagpur region er ekta guruttopurno adibasi folk festival, jekhane Karam gachh ke pujo kora hoy. Ei utsob tarun-taruni der jibon, krishi, shukh-shanti aar paribarer bhalo bhagyo-r protik. Raat e gaan, nritya, aar lokkatha mile ei parab ke ekta rongin anondo te porinoto kore. 🌱

Best Time: August – September (Bhadro Mas) 🌧️

Best Moment: Night Rituals, Songs & Dance 🌙

Famous For: Karam Tree Worship • Folk Songs • Dance

Why Karam Parab is Famous?

  • ✔️ Worship of Sacred Karam Tree 🌳
  • ✔️ Symbol of Youth, Love & Fertility 💚
  • ✔️ Traditional Folk Songs & Dance 🎶
  • ✔️ Strong Bond with Nature & Agriculture 🌾
  • ✔️ Night-long Cultural Celebration 🌙

Before You Experience Karam Parab

Karam Parab mainly gramanchol o adibasi elakay palon kora hoy. Ei din lokera upobash rakhe, Karam gachh er niche pujo kore aar raat bhor gaan-nach e mete thake. Nature-lover o culture-lover der jonno eta ekta mon chhowa anubhob. ✨

📍 Location: Purulia & Chotanagpur Region

🎉 Festival Time: August – September

⚠️ Note: Local rituals & traditions respect korun 🙏
