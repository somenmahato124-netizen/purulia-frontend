Pakhi Pahar

Pakhi Pahar - The Land of Rock Carved Birds

Pakhi Pahar is one of the most iconic tourist attractions in Purulia. Eikhane pora paharer gaye 700+ rock carved pakhi (birds) dekha jaye ja artist Chitta Dey er abhinab srishti. Ei jayga nature, art, heritage ar photography lover der jonno ekta perfect spot. Hills, jungle, rock art aar shanti—sob mile Pakhi Pahar ekta magical experience. 🕊️🌿

Best Time to Visit: August – February 🌥️❄️

Ideal For: Adventure, Photography, Art, Hiking 🎒📸

Famous Things to See in Pakhi Pahar

  • ✔️ 700+ Rock Carved Birds on Mountain Walls
  • ✔️ Forest Trail & Nature Walk Through Hills
  • ✔️ Beautiful Cliff View & Photography Point
  • ✔️ Local Tribal Culture & Handicraft Area
  • ✔️ Adventure Hiking Path & Hidden Spots

Why You Should Visit Pakhi Pahar

Jara ekbar ekhane ashe tara abar fire aste chay. Sobcheye boro beauty holo rock art + nature + silence e tin er combination. Ei jayga tomo shorir mon duita-ke fresh kore dibe.

🚌 Distance: Purulia Town theke 42+ km

⛰️ Location: Baghmundi Region, Ajodhya Hills Area

📸 Must Carry: Camera, Water Bottle, Trek Shoes
