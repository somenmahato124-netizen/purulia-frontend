Bamni Falls

Bamni Falls - The Hidden Waterfall of Ajodhya Hills

Bamni Falls Purulia Ajodhya Hills er moddhe sobcheye sundor natural waterfall. Nicher dike jete hoile trekk korte hoy and path ekdom jungle er moddhe. Jar jonno ei jayga adventure lover der favourite! Pahar, jhorna, pathor ar jungle milaye Bamni Falls ekdom heaven-like experience. 💧🌿

Best Time to Visit: October – March 🍂❄️

Perfect For: Trekking, Photography, Adventure, Nature Trip 🎒📸

Highlights of Bamni Falls

  • ✔️ Deep Jungle Trekking Path
  • ✔️ Multi-layered Waterfall Down the Rock Valley
  • ✔️ Most Photogenic Spot of Ajodhya Hills
  • ✔️ Peaceful & Natural Environment
  • ✔️ Water Sound Echo & Misty Vibes

Things to Know Before Visiting

Bamni Falls e neme jawa rasta steep & bajar moto niche namte hoy, tai comfort shoes pora must. Pani er dhar dhore naamle ekta fresh hawa ar mist chokhe pore. Nijer pani, snacks rakhle valo hoy.

🚌 Distance: Ajodhya Hilltop theke ~7-9 km

⛰️ Difficulty: Medium – Trekking Required

⚠️ Important: Raat e na jawa, jhap jangal & deep slopes
