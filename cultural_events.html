Events

🎭 Chau Dance – Pride of Purulia

Chau Dance holo Purulia’r shobcheye famous traditional dance, jekhane mask, powerful movement ar folk music mile ekta dramatic cultural performance toiri hoy. Ei dance UNESCO recognized ar worldwide famous. 🥁

Season: Chaitra – Baisakh

Famous For: Mask Dance • Folk Music • Tradition

Why Chau Dance is Famous?

  • ✔️ UNESCO Recognized Folk Dance
  • ✔️ Powerful Mask & Martial Moves
  • ✔️ Cultural Identity of Purulia
  • ✔️ Huge Festival Crowd & Stage Shows

🌾 Tusu Parab – Festival of Harvest

Tusu Parab holo Purulia’r ekta popular harvest festival, jekhane young girls Tusu idol baniye gaan, mela ar cultural program kore. Ei parab ta rural joy & folk culture reflect kore. 🎶

Season: Poush – Makar Sankranti

Famous For: Folk Songs • Idol Making • Rural Fair

Why Tusu Parab is Special?

  • ✔️ Harvest Celebration of Villages
  • ✔️ Traditional Songs & Procession
  • ✔️ Colorful Rural Culture
  • ✔️ Community Bonding Event

🌸 Bhadu Parab – Folk Song Festival

Bhadu Parab holo monsoon er shomoy palon kora ekta folk festival, jekhane meyera Bhadu gaan geye culture preserve kore. Ei parab ta love, nature ar folk emotion reflect kore. 💛

Season: Bhadro – Ashwin

Famous For: Folk Songs • Women Participation

🌿 Karam Parab – Tribal Worship Festival

Karam Parab holo tribal community’r ekta important festival, jekhane Karam tree pujo kore bhalo fasol ar bhalo jibon er ashirbad chawa hoy. Dance, song ar rituals ei parab er main attraction. 🌳

Season: Monsoon

Famous For: Tribal Dance • Ritual • Nature Worship

🥁 Jhumur Song & Dance – Soul of Purulia

Jhumur gaan o nach holo Purulia’r daily folk life’r part, jekhane love, hardship ar nature’r kotha gaan diye bola hoy. Ei cultural form ta Purulia’r heart bole mana hoy. ❤️

Season: Throughout the Year

Famous For: Folk Songs • Local Instruments
