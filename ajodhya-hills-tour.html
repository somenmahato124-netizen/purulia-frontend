Ayodhya Hills Tour

Ayodhya Hills, Purulia

Tour Overview

Ayodhya Hills Tour is a refreshing hill getaway in the heart of Purulia. Surrounded by lush forests, rocky hills and waterfalls, this tour is perfect for nature lovers, couples, photographers and adventure seekers looking for scenic beauty and peaceful vibes.

Tour Highlights

⛰️ Ayodhya Pahar View Point

🦚 Mayur Pahar

🗿 Pakhi Pahar

💦 Turga Falls

🌄 Scenic Hill Views

📷 Photography & Nature Walk

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Hill Exploration

Pick up from Purulia / Barabhum Railway Station → Drive to Ayodhya Hills → Hotel / homestay check-in → Visit Ayodhya Pahar View Point → Explore Mayur Pahar → Evening leisure → Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 2: Waterfall & Art Site Visit

Early morning nature walk → Breakfast → Visit Pakhi Pahar → Drive to Turga Falls → Relax & photography → Return journey → Drop at Purulia / Barabhum Railway Station.
