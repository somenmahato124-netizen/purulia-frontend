Ajodhya Hills & Baranti Combo Tour

Ayodhya Hills, Pakhi Pahar, Turga Falls & Baranti Lake, Purulia

Tour Overview

The Ajodhya Hills & Baranti Combo Tour (3 Days / 2 Nights) brings together the scenic hill views of Ayodhya Pahar and the serene lakeside charm of Baranti. Designed for couples, families, photographers, and nature lovers, this package offers a seamless blend of adventure, waterfalls, and peaceful sunset views.

Tour Highlights

⛰️ Ayodhya Pahar View Point & Upper Dam
🌊 Baranti Hill Lake Sunset View
🗿 Pakhi Pahar Rock Sculptures
💦 Turga Falls & Bamni Falls
🧱 Muradi Dam & Garh Panchakot
📷 Nature & Landscape Photography

Detailed Itinerary

Day 01

Arrival & Ayodhya Hills Sightseeing

Pick up from Purulia / Barabhum Railway Station → Drive through scenic forest roads to Ayodhya Hills → Hotel / homestay check-in → Visit Ayodhya Pahar View Point, Upper & Lower Dam → Explore Pakhi Pahar → Evening leisure → Dinner & overnight stay at Ayodhya Hills.

Day 02

Waterfalls Exploration & Transfer to Baranti

Breakfast at hotel → Visit Turga Falls & Bamni Falls → Scenic drive towards Baranti → Check-in at lakeside resort / homestay → Walk around Baranti Lake & enjoy mesmerizing sunset views → Dinner & overnight stay at Baranti.

Day 03

Muradi Dam, Garh Panchakot & Departure

Early morning sunrise & lakeside walk → Breakfast → Visit Muradi Dam & historic Garh Panchakot foothills → Leisure time for local shopping → Return journey & drop at Purulia / Adra / Asansol Railway Station.
