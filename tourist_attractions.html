Attractions

🏔️ Ajodhya Pahar – Heart of Purulia Tourism

Ajodhya Pahar holo Purulia’r shobcheye famous tourist destination, jekhane pahari landscape, dam, waterfall, jungle ar sunset point mile ekta complete nature experience pawa jay. Adventure lover, photographer ar nature traveler der jonno eta ekta must-visit spot. 🌄

Best Time: Winter & Monsoon

Famous For: Hills • Dam • Sunset • Trekking

Why Ajodhya Pahar is Famous?

  • ✔️ Best Hill Station of Purulia
  • ✔️ Multiple Dams & Waterfalls
  • ✔️ Scenic Sunset & Sunrise
  • ✔️ Adventure & Nature Photography

🌊 Baranti Lake – Calm Water Paradise

Baranti Lake holo Purulia’r ekta peaceful lake destination, jekhane water reflection, hill shadow ar silent atmosphere mind relax korte help kore. Family trip, couple travel & photography er jonno eta onek popular. 💙

Best Time: Winter Morning & Evening

Famous For: Lake View • Sunrise • Resort Stay

🛕 Garpanchakot – Temple & Heritage Hill

Garpanchakot holo temple, fort ruins ar hill view er ekta unique combination, jekhane spirituality ar history nature’r sathe mile jai. Weekend trip er jonno eta ekta perfect destination. 🏞️

Best Time: Winter & Post Monsoon

Famous For: Temple • Ruins • Hill View

🏛️ Pakbirra – Ancient Jain Temples

Pakbirra holo Purulia’r ekta historical Jain temple complex, jekhane ancient stone sculpture ar religious heritage dekha jay. History lover & cultural explorer der jonno eta ekta important destination. 🗿

Best Time: Winter

Famous For: Jain Temples • Archaeology
