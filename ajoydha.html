Ayodhya Hills

The Crown Jewel of Purulia – Where Misty Peaks Meet Cascading Waterfalls

Destination Overview

Ayodhya Pahar – The Crown of Purulia

Ayodhya Hills (Ajodhya Pahar) is the most iconic tourist destination in Purulia district, forming a scenic extension of the Dalma Hills and Eastern Ghats range. Surrounded by dense Sal and Palash forests, rocky cliffs, tribal villages, roaring waterfalls, and serene dams, it is a paradise for nature lovers, trekkers, and photographers.

Whether watching the golden sunrise from Mayur Pahar or enjoying the cool mist near Bamni Falls, every corner of Ayodhya Hills offers an unforgettable escape into nature.

Best Time to Visit: October to March (Winter & Spring Palash Bloom) ❄️

Top Activities: Trekking, Camping, Photography, Sightseeing & Rock Climbing 🔥

Must-Visit Attractions

Famous Spots in the Ayodhya Region

The Ayodhya Hills circuit is packed with breathtaking natural wonders and cultural landmarks. Make sure to include these top spots in your itinerary:

  • Bamni Falls: A stunning multi-tiered waterfall cascading through giant rocks.
  • Turga Dam & Waterfall: Ideal for peaceful evening walks and nature photography.
  • Pakhi Pahar (Murraburu): Famous for giant bird sculptures carved directly into rocky cliffs.
  • Mayur Pahar: Offers panoramic sunrise and sunset views over the entire valley.
  • Upper & Lower Dam (PPSP): Scenic reservoirs surrounded by lush green hills.
  • Charida Mask Village: Explore the birthplace of famous Purulia Chhau dance masks.

Nearest Railway Station

Barabhum (BBM) is ~22 km away, and Purulia Junction (PRR) is ~42 km away. Cabs are easily available from both stations.

Stay & Accommodation

Multiple eco-resorts, hilltop cottages, youth hostels, and budget homestays are available at Ayodhya Hilltop and Baghmundi foothills.

Recommended Duration

A 2 Days / 1 Night or 3 Days / 2 Nights trip is ideal to cover the entire Ayodhya Hills circuit comfortably.
