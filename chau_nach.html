Chau Nach

🎭 Chhau Dance – The Soul of Purulia Culture

Chhau Nach holo Purulia'r shobcheye famous o UNESCO-recognized traditional dance form, jekhane mask, powerful body movement, martial art aar mythological stories mile ekta dramatic performance toiri hoy. Ei nach Ramayan, Mahabharat aar local folklore er golpo stage e jibonto kore tule. 🎶🕺

Best Time: Chaitra Parab & Winter Season (March – April) 🌸

Best Moment: Night Performance with Live Music 🎵

Famous For: Mask Dance • Martial Moves • Folk Culture

Why Chhau Dance is Famous?

  • ✔️ UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage 🌍
  • ✔️ Unique Masks & Colorful Costumes 🎭
  • ✔️ Powerful Martial-Art Based Movements ⚔️
  • ✔️ Mythological & Folk Storytelling 📜
  • ✔️ Cultural Pride of Purulia District ❤️

Before You Experience Chhau

Chhau Nach mostly night-time e open stage-e perform hoy, especially village mela aar Chaitra Parab somoy. Live dhol, shehnai aar deep rhythm performance-ke aro powerful kore tole. Ei dance dekha mane Purulia'r culture feel kora. ✨

📍 Location: Purulia District, West Bengal

🎉 Festival: Chaitra Parab, Local Melas

⚠️ Note: Night show – crowd & timing age check korun
