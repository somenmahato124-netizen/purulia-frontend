🌅 Baranti Lake – Purulia

Baranti Lake holo Purulia'r ekta shanto jheel-side destination, jekhane pahar, waterbody ar hawa mile ekta natural peaceful environment toiri hoy. Sunrise theke sunset porjonto ei jaigar light effect mon-ke shanti dey, ar couple, family, photography sobar jonno perfect spot. ✨

Best Time: Winter, Rainy – August to February 🍃

Best Moment: Sunset view over the lake 🌅

Famous For: Quiet Nature • Photography • Sunset • Lakeside Walk

Why Baranti Lake is Famous?

  • ✔️ Sunset reflection over the lake 🌅
  • ✔️ Hills + Waterbody + Nature combo 🍃
  • ✔️ Romantic & peaceful photoshoot spot 📸
  • ✔️ Picnic spot & family-friendly place 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
  • ✔️ Resorts available nearby for night stay 🏡

Before You Visit

Baranti Lake er surrounding calm & clean. Lakeside walk, sunset view ar nature feel korar jonno ekdom perfect. Roads smooth, parking space ache, ar nearby resort gulo comfortable. Nature lovers der jonno pure peaceful destination. ✨

🚘 Distance: Saltora or Santuri road side route

📍 Location: Baranti, Purulia – West Bengal

⚠️ Best Time: Evening Sunset or Early Morning 🌤️
