Joychandi Pahar

🏞️ Joychandi Pahar – Heritage Hill of Purulia

Joychandi Pahar holo Purulia’r ekta iconic viewpoint, jekhane pahar, mandir, nature trail & cinematic landscape ek sathe feel kora jay. 480+ steps uthe hilltop e pouchale pura city & green valley'r panoramic view dekhte pawa jay. Chhau culture, temples, sunrise & photography er jonno onek famous sthan. 🌄📸

Best Time: Morning / Winter Season (October – February) ❄️

Best Moment: Sunrise from Hilltop 🌅

Famous For: Trek • Viewpoint • Temple • Photography

Why Joychandi Pahar Is Famous?

  • ✔️ 480+ Steps Trekking Experience 🪜
  • ✔️ Hilltop Temple & Amazing Valley View 🏞️
  • ✔️ Perfect for Photography & Reels 📸
  • ✔️ Cultural & Historical Importance (Chhau & Heritage) 🎭
  • ✔️ Nearby Park, View Tower & Picnic Spot 🍃

Before You Visit

Stairway trek onek easy, kids & elders o jete pare. Hilltop e pani, chhaya & boshar jayga ache. Winter & morning time e total experience shera. Sunset-o gorgeous lage, but niche aste somoy careful thakun. ✨

🚘 Distance: Raghunathpur – Purulia Road

📍 Location: Joychandi Hill, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Comfortable shoes & water bottle niye jan 👟💧
