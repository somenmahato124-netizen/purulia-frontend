Telkupi Temple

🛕 Telkupi Temple – Lost Heritage of Purulia

Telkupi Temple holo Purulia'r ek prachin historical site, jekhane submerged temple ruins, lake view & ancient structure mile ekta mystic atmosphere toiri hoy. Dam er pani barle kichu mandir pani te dubey thake, r winter e onek part dekhte paoya jay. Ei jayga history lover, explorer & peaceful traveler der jonno perfect. 🌊🏛️

Best Time: Winter Season (November – February) 🍂

Best Moment: Morning Lake View + Temple Ruins 🌅

Famous For: Submerged Temples • Lake • Ancient Ruins • Peaceful Vibe

Why Telkupi Temple is Famous?

  • ✔️ Submerged Ancient Temples 🌊
  • ✔️ Historical & Archaeological Value 📜
  • ✔️ Lake + Temple View = Unique Scenic Spot 🌄
  • ✔️ Peaceful & Less Crowded Location 🕊️
  • ✔️ Photography & Research Friendly 📸

Before You Visit

Telkupi e jawar rasta village-side route, bike/car friendly. Water level er upor base temples sometimes dekhte paoya jay, so winter is best. Shanti, lake breeze, historical ruins — sob mile ekta “lost heritage” feel dey. ✨

🚘 Distance: Near Panchet / Barakar Region

📍 Location: Telkupi, Purulia – West Bengal

⚠️ Note: Water level vary kore — safety & respect maintained rakhun 🙏
