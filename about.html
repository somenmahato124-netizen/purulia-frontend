ABOUT PURULIA DISTRICT

Purulia district is one of the twenty-three districts of West Bengal in Eastern India, with Purulia serving as its administrative headquarters. Major towns include Raghunathpur-Adra, Manbazar, Jhalda, Anara, and Balarampur.

Geography & Climate

Located in the westernmost part of West Bengal, Purulia features undulating Chota Nagpur Plateau terrain with rich red lateritic soil. It experiences warm summers (25–45°C), pleasant winters (7–20°C), and moderate monsoon rainfall (1100–1500mm annually).

The district is famous for its scenic hills like Ajodhya, Joychandi, Panchet, and Baghmundi ranges, along with picturesque waterfalls including Bamni and Turga Falls.

History & Culture

Purulia holds a rich historical legacy dating back to ancient Jain and medieval eras, evident in archaeological sites like Telkupi, Deulghata, and Pakbirra temples. The region is globally renowned for the vibrant, UNESCO-recognized Chhau dance and traditional Jhumur folk songs.

The district has a strong indigenous heritage with Santhal, Bhumij, Kurmi, and Munda communities preserving their vibrant festivals like Tusu, Karam, and Bandna.

Economy & Tourism

Purulia's economy is primarily agrarian with paddy cultivation as the mainstay, supported by lac production, tusser sericulture, and traditional mask-making handicrafts in Charida village. Today, eco-tourism and cultural tourism are flourishing rapidly due to Purulia's serene dams, palash-blooming springs, and rich tribal heritage.
