Tusu Parab

🌾 Tusu Parab – Festival of Harvest & Joy

Tusu Parab holo Purulia o Paschimanchal er ekta popular folk harvest festival, jekhane gram-banglar meye ra Tusu Devi'r pujo kore, gaan gaye aar rangin murti (Tusu idol) niye procession ber hoy. Ei parab notun fasol, shanti aar gram-sanskritir utsob hisabe palon kora hoy. 🎶🌸

Best Time: Poush Sankranti (December – January) ❄️

Best Moment: Village Procession & Folk Songs 🎵

Famous For: Tusu Songs • Harvest Culture • Folk Art

Why Tusu Parab is Famous?

  • ✔️ Traditional Harvest Festival of Purulia 🌾
  • ✔️ Folk Songs Sung by Village Girls 🎶
  • ✔️ Beautiful Tusu Idol & Decorations 🎨
  • ✔️ River Immersion & Procession 🚶‍♀️
  • ✔️ Deep Connection with Rural Life ❤️

Before You Experience Tusu Parab

Tusu Parab mainly gramanchal-e palon kora hoy. Meye ra dol-bodol bhabe Tusu gaan gaye, ghore-ghore ber hoy aar parab er sheshe nodi te Tusu idol bisarjon hoy. Ei utsob Purulia'r lokosanskritir ekta shera porichoy. ✨

📍 Location: Rural Areas of Purulia, West Bengal

🎉 Festival Time: Poush Sankranti

⚠️ Note: Village customs & culture respect korun 🙏
