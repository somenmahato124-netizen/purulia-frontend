🏡 Choridah Chowmask Village – Rural Life of Purulia

Choridah Chowmask Village holo Purulia’r ekta traditional & cultural village, jekhane local manush, mati’r bari, green field aar shanto environment mile ekta pure rural Bengal er feel pawa jay. Ei gram ta folk culture, local lifestyle & simple village life experience er jonno onek famous. 🌾

Best Time: Winter & Post Monsoon (October – February) 🍃

Best Moment: Morning Village Walk & Sunset 🌅

Famous For: Rural Culture • Simple Life • Photography

Why This Village is Special?

  • ✔️ Pure Village Life Experience 🌾
  • ✔️ Traditional Houses & Local People
  • ✔️ Calm, Clean & Pollution-free Area 🍃
  • ✔️ Photography & Documentary Friendly 📸
  • ✔️ Near Forest & Nature Zone

Before You Visit

Village e jawar rasta simple & bike/car friendly. Morning time e gram ta beshi lively lage. Local manush der sathe kotha bole, culture dekhe Purulia’r asli rural charm feel kora jay. ✨

🚘 Distance: Near Purulia Town Area

📍 Location: Choridah Chowmask Village, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Local culture respect korben 🙏
