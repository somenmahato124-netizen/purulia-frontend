Deulghata Temple

🛕 Deughata Temple – Peaceful Spiritual Corner of Ajodhya Hills

Deughata Temple holo Ajodhya Pahar er ekta silent holy spot, jekhane mandir, ghanta dhoni, aar hill er hawa mile ekta shanto atmosphere toiri hoy. Morning worship, peaceful sitting & positive vibe er jonno onek bhalo jayga. Nature + Devotion ei duitar combination ek sathe feel kora jay. 🙏🌿

Best Time: Morning Light & Evening Aarti 🕯️

Best Moment: Mountain Wind + Temple Bells 🌄

Famous For: Peace • Worship • Nature • Spiritual Calm

Why Deughata Temple is Famous?

  • ✔️ Peaceful mountain-side temple 🛕
  • ✔️ Spiritual environment & clean surroundings 🕊️
  • ✔️ Photography & nature sitting spot 📸
  • ✔️ Fresh air + greenery + devotional atmosphere 🍃
  • ✔️ Perfect for prayer, meditation & relaxation

Before You Visit

Deughata Temple e jawar road bike/car friendly. Temple er pashe boshar jayga ache, jekhane hawa lagate lagate shanti feel hoy. Religious place bole silence & respect maintain korte hoy. ✨

🚘 Distance: Ajodhya Hills Road Region

📍 Location: Deughata Temple Side, Purulia

⚠️ Note: Mandir area clean & quiet rakhun – respect place 🙏
